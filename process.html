Start process plan

A clear path from idea to operating business.

Every project starts with scope and priorities. Then we sequence company setup, brand, technology, launch and ongoing operations around what actually needs to happen first.

Six stages

01–02Discover & plan
03–04Set up & build
05–06Launch & scale

01. Discovery Call

Understand the business idea, founder goals, audience, market, timelines and constraints.

  • Goal definition
  • Target market
  • Required services
  • Risk and dependency review

02. Strategy & Blueprint

Turn the idea into a practical roadmap with positioning, priorities, deliverables and sequence.

  • Brand direction
  • Service roadmap
  • Launch architecture
  • Timeline and responsibilities

03. Brand & Company Setup

Create the identity and coordinate the legal/company setup path where required.

  • Name and identity
  • Domain and email
  • Website planning
  • Formation coordination

04. Build & Automate

Build the digital operating layer needed for launch and repeatable execution.

  • Website
  • CRM
  • AI assistant
  • WhatsApp and workflow automation

05. Launch & Growth

Prepare channels, campaigns, sales materials and optional Web3 launch support.

  • Social setup
  • Creative assets
  • Lead generation
  • Launch operations

06. Operate & Scale

Continue with monthly support so systems and execution do not stop after launch day.

  • Customer support
  • CRM follow-ups
  • Reports and optimisation
  • Ongoing admin coordination

Start with the blueprint, not the tools.

We can begin with a discovery session and define exactly what should be built, what should wait, and where specialist partners are needed.
