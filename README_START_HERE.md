## Before going live
1. Create `hello@metaoneclick.com` or replace it throughout the site.
2. Replace the temporary email-based contact form with your CRM/Formspree/HubSpot/Zoho/Netlify form endpoint.
3. Add actual company legal name, registration details and physical/registered address once finalised.
4. Have the Privacy Policy and Terms reviewed for your legal entity and target markets.
5. Do not advertise UAE/free-zone setups as guaranteed “tax-free”. Tax depends on facts and law.
6. For legal/tax/accounting/company-formation work, show the qualified partner/provider where required.
7. For crypto/Web3 work, add your legal/compliance workflow and do not promise listings, token value or investment returns.
8. Add real social links and only add case studies/testimonials/metrics that you can substantiate.
9. Connect analytics and Search Console after deployment.
10. Add a real booking calendar once you choose the provider.

## Suggested launch process for MetaOneClick itself
### Week 1 — Foundation
- Finalise legal entity / operating jurisdiction
- Set up business email
- Set up CRM pipeline
- Define service scopes and partner network
- Set client onboarding documents and proposal template

### Week 2 — Productise the services
Create 4 initial offers:
- Launch Sprint
- Global Setup
- AI Stack
- Operate & Scale

Keep Web3 as a specialist custom-scope offer until the compliance and technical review workflow is fully documented.

### Week 3 — Go live
- Deploy metaoneclick.com
- Connect lead form and calendar
- Add analytics
- Publish 6–10 high-quality launch posts
- Prepare one sample client journey / demo project instead of inventing case studies

### Week 4 — First clients
- Founder outreach
- UAE/India business communities
- LinkedIn founder targeting
- Referral partners: accountants, lawyers, company-formation providers, developers and marketing specialists
- Offer a paid discovery/blueprint or a defined launch sprint rather than “everything for everyone” on day one
